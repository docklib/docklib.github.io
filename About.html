How it started

Hi, I'm Bobby. On 20 October 2022 I began building a games page for myself, because the sites I tried showed a flood of ads while I played. I wanted one page where I could just play. That small page grew into the Sz-Games.com library you see today.

Where the name comes from

There is no hidden meaning. I picked "Sz" because it is short and hard to forget, and the name stuck.

How games get added

Most new games come from players. If something you like is missing, ask for it through the request form or on our Discord server, and it may turn up in a later update.

What comes next

I'll keep adding games and fixing ones that break. The site needs some ads to pay for itself, but I keep them to as few as I can.
