About Basket Random

Basket Random is a chaotic 2-player basketball game where almost everything is random: the court, the players, even the physics. One round you are dunking on a normal court, the next you are bouncing around on ice or a beach while your long-armed ragdoll players flop everywhere. First to 5 baskets wins the round, then a whole new random matchup starts.

How to play

There is only one button per player, which makes it easy to learn and hard to master. Player 1 presses W and Player 2 presses the Up arrow to jump. Press at the right moment to grab the ball, jump again to shoot, and time your jumps to block your opponent. Matches are short, loud and perfect for rematches.

Controls

Player 1: W to jump / shoot. Player 2: Up arrow to jump / shoot. That is the entire control scheme — timing is everything.

Tips to win

Jump slightly before your opponent to steal the ball mid-air, stay between your rival and the hoop on defense, and remember that every new round changes the court — slippery surfaces reward patient, well-timed jumps over spamming the key.
